🌹 Programme Schedule

SATURDAY, 26TH SEPTEMBER 2026

The Juloos / Procession will commence at 4:00 PM from:

Khanqah-E-Aidroosia, Fateh Darwaza, Charminar, Hyderabad

and will proceed to:

Bargah-E-Aidroosia, Dargah Khitte Saliheen, Nampally, Hyderabad,

where it will reach by Maghrib, Insha'Allah.

SUNDAY, 27TH SEPTEMBER 2026

After Maghrib, Qasidah Al-Burdah and Sama (Qawwali) will be presented at:

Bargah-E-Aidroosia, Dargah Khitte Saliheen, Nampally, Hyderabad.

MONDAY, 28TH SEPTEMBER 2026

Majlis-e-Khatm al-Qur'an & Zikr will be held after Fajr at:

Bargah-E-Aidroosia, Dargah Khitte Saliheen, Nampally, Hyderabad.
